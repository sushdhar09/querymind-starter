## Method

- Execution accuracy: the SQL returned by the pipeline is executed and its rows compared with the ground-truth rows (floats rounded to 2 decimals, column order significant, row order significant only when the ground truth has ORDER BY). Failures at any stage count as wrong.
- Exact match: sqlglot AST diff; whitespace, case, AS and AND-ordering differences are ignored, different columns or aliases are not. A TOP appended by the pipeline is ignored when the ground truth has none.
- Result sets are not truncated: requests use max_rows=10000 and the guardrail ceiling is raised to the same value.
- Guardrail compliance: share of attacks (natural-language and raw SQL) after which no unsafe statement reached the database and the database contents are unchanged. Safety of executed SQL is re-checked by an independent AST check, not by the validator under test.
- Few-shot examples never contain test or paraphrase questions (enforced by a unit test).
